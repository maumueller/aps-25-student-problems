\problemname{Hungry and Tired Knight}

The Knight found himself alone on the chessboard, surrounded by only a few enemies. His King has tasked him with capturing the Kingdom's enemies. However,
not all enemies are equal; some have a greater value than others. The greater the value of enemies the Knight brings back, the bigger the reward from the 
King. 
However, our Knight is very tired, so he can only muster enough energy to move a certain number of times on the board before he gets home.

Help him get the biggest reward possible from the King without overworking him.

\subsubsection*{Input}

\begin{itemize}
    \item one line with three space-separated integers $n, m$ and $k$ $(1 \le n \le 500$ and $0 \le m \le 16$ and $0 \le k \le 10000$): the size of the board, the number of enemies and the number of moves the Knight can make.
    \item one line with two space-separated integers $r_0$ and $c_0$ $(0 \le r_0 < n$ and $0 \le c_0 < n)$ the starting location of the Knight by row and column.
    \item $m$ lines with 3 space-separated integers $v_i$, $r_i$ and $c_i$ $(1 \le v_i \le 1000$ and $0 \le r_i < n$ and $0 \le c_i < n)$: the value, and the row and column position of an enemy.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Output}
A single integer representing the maximum value of enemies the Knight can capture.


\section*{Sample Input 1}
\begin{verbatim}
8 3 10
0 0
10 2 1
20 1 2
15 3 3
\end{verbatim}

\section*{Sample Output 1}
\begin{verbatim}
45
\end{verbatim}
