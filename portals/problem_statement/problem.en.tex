\problemname{Reverse List of Numbers}

You are a mighty mage in the lower depths of a dungeon accompanied by your squad.
The dungeon walls are suddenly caving in and you're all in great danger!
In the room you spot a number of portals exactly matching your squad size.
You need to use your magic to open these portals in order to transport out as many members as possible before it's too late.
The portals are brittle and can only withstand transporting a single person before it becomes unusable.
Time is of the essense and opening the portals one at a time will take too long.
Unfortunately, with your limited capabilities you alone are unable to activate more than one portal at a time.
Luckily for you and your party, there are many interconnected runes laid out on the dungeon floor.
You recognize these from the magic hardware store, as the polarized runes that only send magic in one direction.
These runes will be able to distribute your magic and activate more portals at once.
Each wire connecting the runes can only carry a limited amount of magic before they break.
Find a way to route your magic through the interconnected runes to open the greatest number of portals at once, without breaking them.

\section*{Input}
The mage can only access rune $0$, which is where they will send through their magic.

The input consists of one line containing 3 integers:
The number of runes $5 \leq R \leq 20000$, the number of wires $1 \leq W \leq 60000$, the number of portals $2 \leq P \leq 10000$.
Followed by a single line of $P$ portals.
Followed by $W$ lines each containing 3 integers. $0 \leq A \leq R$, $1 \leq B \leq R$, and $1 \leq C \leq 10000$.
$A$ and $B$ indicating a wire between either two runes or a rune and a portal, and $C$ representing the maximum magic the wire can transport before breaking.

\section*{Output}
The output consists of a single line containing the number of portals you are able to open.
