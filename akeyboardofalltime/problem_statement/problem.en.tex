\problemname{a keyboard of all time}

\parindent0pt

they think I don’t know what they’re doing. But I see the them. I hear the keys
at night. The clackclackclack like teeth in the walls. \\
i need to know. who types?? clakc clack clack \\

the voices provide me with information about their keyboard and what they will
type. BUT WHO TYPES WHAT??? \\
the feds are after me, if i cant provide them this information they WILL put me
in a squad car and place me in guantanamo.

every step, one person can choose to make a move. this move is either to jump on
a key to press it, or travel to another connected key. It is not possible for
both people to make a move in the same step.

\begin{Input}
  The input consists of:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item One line containing two integers $N$ ($1 \leq N \leq 26$) and $M$,
          the total number of keys and connected key pairs respectively.
    \item Then follow $M$ lines. Each line contains two space-separated
          letters, $a_i$ and $b_i$, representing two connected keys on the
          keyboard. Each key may either be a letter, or a sequence of
          numbers, the second case indicating a malfunctional key that
          serves no purpose. In either case, they are unique to the key.
    \item Then follows two space-reparated letters, $a_s$ and $b_s$, the
          starting key of person A and person B.
    \item Finally comes a line that contains the string $W$ ($1 \leq
          |W| \leq 500$), the word that the people are going to type. This
          line is guaranteed to only include characters that are on the
          keyboard, and contains no white-space.
  \end{itemize}
\end{Input}

\begin{Output}
Output a line containing a string of the same length as the word $W$, where each
character is either `A` or `B`, indicating which person typed each corresponding
character in $W$. Then, output a second line containing an integer indicating
the total number of steps taken to type the word.
\end{Output}
